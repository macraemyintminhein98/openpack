# Questions for a lawyer

Take these to a lawyer who knows Myanmar law (and, for the maintainer, the law where the
maintainer lives). They are questions, not answers.

## Distribution inside Myanmar

1. Is any registration, licence or permission needed to distribute educational and health
   material on storage devices or over local Wi-Fi? Does it differ for a shop, a school, a
   religious institution or an individual?
2. Which current laws on electronic communications, publishing, printing and telecommunications
   could apply to (a) the maintainer, (b) a shop that copies packs, (c) a person carrying one?
3. Does running a local Wi-Fi hotspot that serves files to nearby phones count as providing a
   telecommunications or internet service?
4. Are there categories of content that are lawful elsewhere but restricted in Myanmar, which
   an encyclopedia such as Wikipedia is likely to contain? What is the exposure for
   distributing it unedited?
5. What should a carrier say and show if asked what is on their device?

## Health content

6. Is there any liability for publishing first-aid guidance that follows WHO and Red Cross
   material but was not written by a licensed professional? Does a visible "not reviewed by
   an expert" notice change that?
7. Once a health professional reviews a lesson, does naming them create liability for them?

## Copyright and licences

8. Are Creative Commons licences enforceable and recognised in Myanmar?
9. For non-commercial licences (CC BY-NC): may a shop charge for the copying service or the
   storage card while the content itself is free? Where is the line?
10. What records should the maintainer keep to show permission for each item?

## The maintainer

11. Does publishing the software and packs from abroad under my own name create risk when I
    travel to or live in Myanmar? Should the project be held by an organisation instead?
12. Should the signing key be held by a person or an organisation, and in which country?
13. Is a takedown process as described in `CONTENT-POLICY.md` sufficient?

## Privacy

14. The software keeps no logs and no accounts. Is there anything a hub is nevertheless
    required to record or report?
