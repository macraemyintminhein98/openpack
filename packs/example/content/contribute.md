# How to contribute content

1. Only add content you may legally redistribute (see `lib/licenses.js`).
2. Every item needs a licence and, unless CC0/public domain, an attribution.
3. Content from creators who say yes is welcome: keep the written permission and
   reference it in the `proof` field.
4. No pirated films, music, books or software. The builder will refuse the pack.
5. Keep news neutral and factual.
